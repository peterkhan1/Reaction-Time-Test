01Why Reaction Test exists

Most online tests give you one number and little else. We wanted tests that start in one click, feel fast, and explain what the result means: where you sit on a scale, how steady you were, and whether you are improving.

Everything happens inside one box. You click to begin, play, and see your analysis in the same place, with your history one tap away.

02What you can test

  • Reaction time test: five rounds, average in milliseconds.
  • CPS test: clicks per second over 1 to 60 seconds.
  • Aim trainer: classic, flick, precision, moving and tracking modes.
  • Typing speed test: live WPM and accuracy.
  • Memory test: sequence, number and visual memory.

03How we build them

The tests are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no third-party scripts, so they load quickly and run offline once loaded. Timing uses your browser's high-resolution clock and is anchored to the frame where the signal is actually painted. See how the test works for details.

04What these tests are not

They are practice and comparison tools. Your numbers include display, input device and browser delay, and they are not medical, clinical or laboratory assessments. Our rating labels are benchmarks, not diagnoses.

05Private by design

There are no accounts. Results and history are stored only in your own browser and are never uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy, or contact us with ideas and bug reports.
