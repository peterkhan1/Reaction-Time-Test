# Launch checklist

- [ ] Replace `example.com` and `hello@example.com` everywhere (Ctrl+Shift+H in VS Code)
- [ ] Upload ALL files, including `.htaccess` (hidden file) and the `assets/` folder
- [ ] Open the live site and check: every page loads, fonts look right, no 404 on CSS/JS (browser DevTools -> Network)
- [ ] Turn on SSL (HTTPS), then uncomment the redirect block in `.htaccess`
- [ ] Test on a real phone: reaction test (touch), typing test (keyboard opens), memory tests
- [ ] Open https://yourdomain/not-a-page and confirm the themed 404 appears
- [ ] Submit sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- [ ] Share a page link in WhatsApp/Telegram/X to confirm the preview image shows
- [ ] Read privacy.html and terms.html once. Adjust to your country or business if needed. They are general templates, not legal advice
- [ ] Optional: replace the OG images in `assets/og/` with your own artwork (1200x630 PNG, same file names)
