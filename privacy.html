Privacy Policy

Short version: no accounts, no uploads, no cookies. Your results stay in your browser and you can delete them any time.

In short

  • No account is needed and nothing you do in a test is uploaded.
  • Best scores and history live in your browser's local storage only.
  • You can delete everything in one click, below.

01What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account details to use the tests, and we do not send your test results anywhere. Your scores are calculated and displayed in your browser.

02What is saved in your browser

To show your best scores and history, the site uses your browser's local storage on your own device. These items never leave it:

Saved itemWhat it holds
rt:sess:*Your recent sessions for each test, shown in the History section
rt:rx:*, rt:cps:*, rt:aim:*, rt:ty:*, rt:mem:*Best scores and the last options you chose in each test
rt:themeYour light or dark mode choice

We do not set cookies.

04Third parties

The site loads its fonts and scripts from its own server. It does not use advertising or tracking scripts. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

05Server logs

Like any website, the web server hosting this site may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. We use it only to keep the site running.

06Children

The site is general-purpose and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

07Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, we will update this page and its date.

08Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@example.com or use the contact page.
