Modes

Five aim training modes

Each mode trains a different part of aim. Switch between them inside the box.

Click-based modes

  • Classic: timed. Three targets stay on screen; hit as many as you can. Good general warm-up.
  • Flick: one target at a time after a random pause. Measures how fast you move and land on it, in milliseconds.
  • Precision: small targets, fixed count. Accuracy matters more than speed.
  • Moving: targets drift across the box. Practise leading your shots.

Tracking mode

Keep your cursor on one smoothly moving target. There is no clicking; the score is the percentage of time you stay on target, plus your longest lock and how often you lose it.

Accuracy = hits ÷ (hits + misses). Average time is measured from when a target appears until you hit it, so it includes your search time.

How it works

How scoring works

Every mode ends with a result and a mini graph inside the box.

A run

  1. Choose a mode, target size and length.
  2. Click the box to begin. Your first click starts the run.
  3. Hit targets. Misses show a red cross and count against accuracy.
  4. Review score, accuracy, average time per hit, best streak and a per-second graph.

What affects your aim?

  • Sensitivity: keep one setting long enough to build muscle memory. Your eDPI is mouse DPI × in-game sensitivity.
  • Mouse and pad: a mouse pad big enough for a full turn helps; disable mouse acceleration where you can.
  • Your screen: a higher refresh rate shows targets sooner.
  • Warm-up and fatigue: short, regular sessions beat one long grind.
Practice

How to improve your aim

Browser trainers are a warm-up and a check, not a replacement for your actual game.

Before you train

  • Start with Classic to warm up, then switch to Precision for control.
  • Pick a target size that keeps accuracy near 80 to 90 percent.
  • Use the same mouse and sensitivity every time.

During training

  • Slow down when accuracy drops. Speed follows control.
  • Use Tracking for smoothness and Flick for first-shot speed.
  • Review the graph: steady bars mean steady aim.

Questions

Aim trainer FAQ

What people ask before their first run.

Is this aim trainer free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download and no account.

Which mode is best for FPS games?

Flick and Precision map closest to first-shot accuracy. Tracking is useful for games where you follow targets.

Does mouse sensitivity matter?

Yes. Use the same mouse DPI and a sensitivity you can control. Changing it constantly makes progress hard to measure.

What is a good accuracy?

Above 80 to 90 percent on Medium targets is solid. Small targets are harder, so compare like with like.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, with touch. A mouse gives more accurate and comparable results.

Are my scores saved?

Best scores and history are stored in your browser only.
