Short tests flatter you, because you can sprint for a second or two. Longer tests show what you can hold.

What affects your CPS?

  • Test length: 1 and 5 second tests measure a burst, 30 and 60 seconds measure endurance and usually score lower.
  • Technique: normal clicking is the fair baseline. Jitter and butterfly clicking are faster but tire your hand and some servers restrict them.
  • Your mouse: a light, responsive click helps. Check you are using the same button setting every time.
  • Warm-up and rest: a few short sprints help; stop if your hand or wrist hurts.
How it works

How the CPS test works

CPS means clicks per second: your click count divided by the time you chose.

The test

  1. Pick 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds.
  2. Click the box. Your first click starts the clock and counts as click one.
  3. Keep clicking. Every click shows a ripple so you can see it register.
  4. Read your CPS, total clicks, fastest second, consistency and pace.

How is it scored?

CPS = total clicks ÷ seconds. A 10 second test with 80 clicks is 8.0 CPS.

Consistency compares your seconds against each other, and Pace tells you whether your second half was faster or slower than your first. Switch between Left + right and Left only to match the rules you play by, or choose Space bar to test your keyboard.

Practice

How to click faster

Speed comes from relaxed tension and rhythm, not from squeezing harder.

Set up

  • Use a mouse with a light click and a firm surface.
  • Rest your forearm so your wrist stays neutral.
  • Use the same input settings every time.

Practise

  • Do short 5 second sprints with 30 seconds of rest.
  • Watch the live bars: even bars score better than one big burst.
  • Use the Pace score to see if you fade in the second half.

Questions

CPS test FAQ

Answers to the most common click speed questions.

What does CPS mean?

Clicks per second. It is your number of clicks divided by the length of the test in seconds.

What is a good CPS?

About 6 to 8 CPS is good for normal clicking over 10 seconds. Past 10 CPS usually involves jitter or butterfly clicking.

Which test length should I use?

Use 5 seconds to compare with friends and 10 seconds for a steadier measure. Use 30 or 60 seconds to test endurance.

Does right click count?

By default left and right buttons both count, which allows butterfly clicking. Switch to Left only if you want strict one-button scoring.

Can I test with the Space bar?

Yes. Choose Space bar in the options and press Space instead of clicking.

Why is my score lower than other sites?

Longer tests, a different mouse and strict one-button rules all lower the number. Compare using the same duration and settings.

Are my results saved?

Your best score and history stay in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
