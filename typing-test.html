What affects your typing speed?

  • Accuracy: corrections cost time, so slowing down slightly often raises your net WPM.
  • Word type: punctuation and capitals take extra keystrokes, so scores are usually lower with punctuation on.
  • Keyboard and posture: relaxed wrists and a keyboard you know well help.
  • Practice and rhythm: steady rhythm beats bursts. The graph shows how even you were.
How it works

How the typing test works

Common English words, scored the standard way.

The test

  1. Pick 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds. Add punctuation for a harder test.
  2. Start typing. The first key starts the timer.
  3. Press Space for the next word. Backspace fixes errors, even back into the previous word if it was wrong.
  4. Check WPM, raw WPM, accuracy, word count and a per-second graph.

How is it scored?

WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. A word counts as five characters, including spaces.

Raw WPM counts every character, right or wrong. Accuracy is correct keystrokes ÷ all keystrokes, so errors you later fix still count against it.

Practice

How to type faster

Accuracy first. Speed is what accuracy turns into with practice.

Habits

  • Look at the screen, not the keyboard.
  • Keep your wrists relaxed and fingers curved over the home row.
  • Slow down until errors become rare, then push the speed.

Practice

  • Ten focused minutes a day beat one long session.
  • Turn on punctuation once plain words feel easy.
  • Use the graph: steady bars mean steady rhythm.

Questions

Typing test FAQ

Quick answers about scoring and speed.

What is WPM?

Words per minute. One word is counted as five characters, so WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 to 55 WPM is typical, 55 to 70 is good, and above 70 is fast. Accuracy of 95 percent or more matters as much as speed.

What is the difference between raw and net WPM?

Raw WPM counts all characters typed. The big number is net WPM, which only counts correct characters.

Does punctuation change my score?

Usually it lowers it, because capitals, commas and full stops take extra keystrokes.

Can I restart quickly?

Press Tab, or the Reset button. Esc stops a run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, but a physical keyboard gives results you can compare.

Are results saved?

Your best WPM and history stay in your browser only.
